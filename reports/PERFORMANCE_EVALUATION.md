## Interpretation

The optimized retriever reuses a fitted TF-IDF index and caches repeated query results with TTL and bounded size. The exact-match rate compares returned article IDs with the pre-optimization implementation on the same inputs.
Groq inference latency and token consumption require a separately authorized provider benchmark and are therefore reported as unavailable here.
